Phishing Awareness Quiz

Q1: What is the main goal of phishing attacks?

A) Improve system performance
B) Steal sensitive information
C) Install updates

Answer: B


Q2: Which of the following is a phishing indicator?

A) Suspicious links
B) Strong password
C) Software update

Answer: A


Q3: What should you do when you receive a suspicious email?

A) Click the link immediately
B) Verify the sender and avoid suspicious links
C) Share your password

Answer: B


Q4: Which technique do attackers use to manipulate victims?

A) Social Engineering
B) Encryption
C) Backup

Answer: A
